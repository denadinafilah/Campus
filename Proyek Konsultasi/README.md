# Business Consulting Project

## Overview

An academic project focused on understanding business consulting fundamentals and gaining exposure to industrial supply chain processes.

The final group assignment involved analyzing an online retail dataset. Our team focused on skincare products to explore business-related insights through data analysis.

## Learning Areas

- Introduction to business consulting concepts.
- Understanding supply chain processes and industry fundamentals.
- Exploring online retail business operations.
- Applying data analysis to support business understanding.

## Final Group Assignment: Skincare Retail Data Analysis

The final assignment involved analyzing an online retail dataset with a focus on skincare products.

The project aimed to explore patterns and insights from retail data to better understand business performance and customer-related aspects.

## Key Learning Outcomes

- Developed an understanding of supply chain and retail business fundamentals.
- Gained exposure to data-driven business analysis.
- Practiced collaborative problem-solving through a group project.
- Explored the application of data analysis in a business context.

## Project Information

**Project Type:** Academic Group Project  
**Field:** Business Consulting & Data Analysis
