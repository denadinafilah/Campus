# KOMPOR UPI – Robotics & Automation Community

## Overview

KOMPOR UPI (Komunitas Mahasiswa Pecinta Robot, Otomasi, dan Robotika Universitas Pendidikan Indonesia) is a student community focused on exploring robotics, automation, and related technologies.

This experience provided an opportunity to learn fundamental electronic and control system concepts through technical exploration and collaborative learning.

## Learning Areas

- **Sensors:** Understanding sensor principles and their applications in detecting environmental conditions.
- **Actuators:** Exploring actuators as components that convert control signals into physical actions.
- **Microcontrollers:** Learning the fundamentals of microcontroller-based systems.
- **Serial Communication:** Exploring communication between electronic devices and microcontrollers.

## Key Learning Outcomes

- Developed foundational knowledge of robotics and automation systems.
- Gained exposure to electronic components and their practical applications.
- Explored the relationship between sensing, processing, and actuation.
- Improved technical exploration and collaborative learning skills.
