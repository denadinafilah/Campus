# Computational Materials Science

## Overview

An academic learning experience focused on computational materials science and the application of first-principles simulation methods to explore material properties.

The exploration involved using Quantum ESPRESSO and JDFTx to study selected materials, including gold (Au), silicon (Si), and graphene.

## Computational Tools

- **Quantum ESPRESSO:** An open-source suite for electronic-structure calculations and materials modeling.
- **JDFTx:** A software package for electronic-structure calculations, including density functional theory (DFT).

## Materials Explored

| Material | Chemical Symbol |
|---|---|
| Gold | Au |
| Silicon | Si |
| Graphene | C |

## Learning Focus

- Introduction to computational materials science.
- Understanding the fundamentals of Density Functional Theory (DFT).
- Exploring first-principles computational approaches.
- Learning the use of Quantum ESPRESSO and JDFTx for materials simulations.
- Exploring the computational characteristics of different materials.

## Key Learning Outcomes

- Developed foundational knowledge of computational physics and materials modeling.
- Gained experience with DFT-based simulation software.
- Explored the application of computational methods to different material systems.
